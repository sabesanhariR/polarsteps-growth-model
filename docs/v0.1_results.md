## Sensitivity (top drivers)

Ranked by Spearman rank correlation with the outcome.

1. addressable_m (-0.73)
2. active_share (-0.51)
3. conversion (+0.44)
4. invites_per_trip (-0.40)
5. paid_share_start (+0.16)
6. paid_decay (+0.08)

_Caveat_: after calibration the loop inputs trade off against each other. A large addressable market forces smaller active_share and invites to match the observed counts, which is why addressable_m, active_share and invites have negative signs. Read the ranking as "what the result is most sensitive to", not as the effect of increasing each input.

## Known limitations at v0.1

- Counts cumulative users, not active users.
- Holdout falls outside the predicted p10 to p90 band.
- Constant growth rate against back-loaded history.
- World multiplier is a placeholder, so the reach probabilities are conditional on it.
- Invites per trip (about 5) is from press reports and is not verified.
- Outside-in model on public data; not a forecast.
